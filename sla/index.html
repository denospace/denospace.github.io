Service commitments

These apply to the hosted service, which is not open yet. Self-hosted instances are yours to run and are not covered — though nothing here is hidden from you either.

Availability

99.5% uptime, measured monthly, excluding announced maintenance. That is roughly three and a half hours a month. We are deliberately not claiming more: Denospace is run by a very small team, and a number we can actually hold is worth more than an impressive one we can't.

Scheduled maintenance is announced at least 48 hours ahead and kept to low-traffic hours. Unscheduled downtime is reported on the status page as it happens, not afterwards.

If we miss it

Below 99.5% in a month, a 25% credit for that month. Below 95%, a full credit. You ask and we apply it — or cancel, and your notes are unaffected either way, which is rather the point.

Your data

The canonical copy of your notes is yours and always was. It lives in your own storage, in a folder you control, as plain Org files. We hold a working copy to serve and index.

So there is no export feature and no need for one. If you cancel, if we fail, if you simply change your mind, your notes are already where they were. This is the most important commitment on this page and it is structural rather than contractual — it is true because of how the thing is built.

Backups

Our working copy and index are backed up daily and kept 30 days. These exist so we can recover the service, not so we can recover your notes — your notes are in your own storage.

Support

By email, best effort, with a target of two business days for a first reply. Not 24/7, and we will not pretend otherwise.

If we shut down

90 days' notice, and the server stays MIT licensed and self-hostable throughout, so there is a path that does not involve us. Given that your files never left your storage, a shutdown costs you a running service, not a library.

Security

TLS in transit. The hosted service requests access to a single folder in your storage, not your whole account. Credentials are not logged. Any breach affecting your data is disclosed within 72 hours of our becoming aware of it.

Changes

Material reductions to these commitments take effect no sooner than 30 days after they are published, and are announced rather than quietly edited.
